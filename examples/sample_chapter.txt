Chapter 1: The Beginning of the Journey

The morning sun cast long golden shadows across the kingdom of Aincrad. 
Kirito tightened his cloak as he stood at the edge of the town square.

"Are you ready, partner?" asked Asuna, adjusting the silver rapier at her waist. 
The bustling crowd of adventurers murmured in excitement as the grand guild gates opened.

"Always," Kirito replied with a faint smile. They both knew that beyond the safe zone, 
the monsters roaming the Great Plains were far more ferocious than anything they had encountered before.
